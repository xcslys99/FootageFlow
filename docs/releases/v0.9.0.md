# FootageFlow v0.9.0 — Research & Culture Update

FootageFlow now combines media discovery with a local project research workspace.

## Added

- Media / Research / All search modes
- Wikipedia, Wikidata, The Met, Art Institute of Chicago, Crossref, and GDELT research discovery
- Project Research Notes with My Note, tags, citations, refresh, and Find Related Media
- Source-confirmed **Add as Media** for public-domain Met/AIC records that include a public original-image URL
- Research References and Combined Project Report exports
- Portable project schema 2 with research references and backward-compatible schema 1 import

## Important limits

- Research records are references, not pre-cleared media.
- Crossref provides metadata; FootageFlow does not download restricted paper full text.
- GDELT is discovery-only; FootageFlow does not download or clear news articles or images.
- A Wikipedia preview must be traced to its specific Wikimedia Commons file and license before reuse.
- Museum rights are shown only when provider metadata explicitly indicates them.

## Assets

- `FootageFlow-0.9.0-macOS-arm64.dmg` — macOS 15+ on Apple Silicon

- `FootageFlow-0.9.0-Windows-x64-portable.zip` — Windows 11 x64 portable package

FootageFlow remains local-first, has no account or analytics, and never forces an update.
